## Going for gaps in glaucoma

Vision loss is growing as the population ages. EyeTRU works to prevent visual impairment and
improve quality of life for people with eye diseases, with a particular focus on glaucoma.

We bridge disciplines, from cell biology and pharmacology to clinical ophthalmology, epidemiology
and machine learning. We work closely as a team and with a wide network of national and
international collaborators, patient organisations and industry partners.

Our largest initiative is [Project FOREVER](/forever/), a Danish eye and vision cohort built
together with Synoptik optician stores across Denmark.
