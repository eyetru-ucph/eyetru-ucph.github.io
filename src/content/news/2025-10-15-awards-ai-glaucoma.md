Our collaboration with DTU on trustworthy artificial intelligence for glaucoma screening was
recognised twice in 2025:

- **Best Poster Award** at UNSURE 2025, held at MICCAI 2025, for *Uncertainty-Aware
  Classification: A Human-Guided Bayesian Deep Learning Framework* (Kampen, Reimann et al.).
- **Best Rapid Fire Award** at the EVER congress for *Ensuring Reliable Glaucoma Detection with
  Deep Learning: The Critical Role of Calibration and Evaluation Practices* (Reimann et al.).
