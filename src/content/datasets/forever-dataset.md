Applications are reviewed by the FOREVER steering group. Approved projects sign a data access
agreement and work in a secure compute environment with an NVIDIA A100 GPU; data cannot be
copied off the server. Please cite the dataset and the design paper
(doi:10.1111/aos.15693) when you publish.
