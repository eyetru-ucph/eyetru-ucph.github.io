## About FOREVER

FOREVER is a public-private collaboration between the University of Copenhagen and
Synoptik A/S, supervised by an international consortium. People who have an advanced eye
examination at a Synoptik store across Denmark are invited to take part. With their consent,
examination data, retinal images and an online questionnaire are linked to genetic
information and to the Danish National Health Registries.

### Aims

1. **Identify risk factors and predictors of visual disability**, such as glaucoma,
   age-related macular degeneration and diabetic eye disease.
2. **Find signs of systemic disease in the eye**, to help prevent life-threatening conditions.
3. **Validate the eye health check in community optometry**, so opticians and
   ophthalmologists can work better together.

### Two cohorts

- **Selected cohort:** people aged 50 and over with an earlier fundus photograph are invited
  by email, give a saliva sample in store and answer the questionnaire online.
- **Extended cohort:** adults aged 18 and over are recruited in store after an advanced eye
  examination and receive the invitation and questionnaire by email.

FOREVER also powers our work on artificial intelligence for glaucoma screening, together with
the Technical University of Denmark.
